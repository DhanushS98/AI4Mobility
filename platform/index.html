Turning NHS physiotherapy guidance into pictures, sound & live feedback

A prototype assistive platform that helps parents and caregivers guide children with mobility difficulties through home exercises correctly and confidently — grounded in trusted NHS resources.

Why this exists

Children with conditions such as cerebral palsy, developmental delay, or those recovering from injury often need regular physiotherapy at home. NHS guidance is thorough but text-heavy — hard to follow for non-specialists, and less accessible for children, low-literacy readers, or non-native speakers. This prototype converts written exercise instructions into step-by-step illustrations, spoken narration, and live camera feedback.

Exercise guide

Step-by-step illustrated instructions generated from NHS exercise descriptions, with optional spoken narration for hands-free or low-vision use. Every exercise keeps its original wording, and you can edit it or paste your own description to rebuild the steps.

Uses your device camera and an in-browser pose-detection model to give simple real-time cues while practising the step you have selected above. The target joint angles come from the same illustrations shown in the guide. All processing happens locally in your browser; no video is uploaded anywhere.

About & grounding sources

This prototype maps directly onto the AI4Mobility proposal: transforming trusted clinical guidance into accessible multimodal formats.

1. Text → Steps

Exercise descriptions are separated into ordered steps, with repetitions, hold times and safety notes identified automatically. Any description can be edited or replaced and re-converted.

2. Steps → Visual

Each step is rendered as a simplified illustrated diagram from a library of 46 hand-built poses, a stand-in for a generative vision model in this prototype.

3. Visual → Speech

Each step, or the whole guide, can be read aloud using your browser's built-in text-to-speech, standing in for an open-source TTS pipeline.

4. Personalisation

Condition and difficulty selections adjust which exercises are recommended and their suggested reps and hold times, with the NHS default always shown alongside.

5. Live pose feedback

An in-browser pose-estimation model tracks joints in real time and compares elbow, knee, hip and trunk angles with the illustrated target for the current step.

Prototype status

This is a working demo built to test the concept end-to-end. It is not a certified medical device and should not replace advice from a qualified physiotherapist.
