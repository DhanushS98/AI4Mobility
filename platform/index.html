Select a programme to load its description, or open any programme and replace the text with your own. The description is separated into numbered steps, each shown with an illustration and, if you turn the camera on, live feedback on your position.

AI4Mobility — Project A60, MSc AI Research and Development Project (55-708252), Sheffield Hallam University.

Exercise content adapted with attribution from General lower limb exercises, Core and hip stability exercises, Glute strengthening, Calf control exercises and Wrist exercises (Sheffield Children's NHS Foundation Trust), and Yoga Basics — Threading the Needle.

The camera runs entirely on this device. No image is uploaded, stored or recorded. This is a student project, not a medical device, and not a substitute for advice from a qualified physiotherapist. Stop any exercise that causes pain and seek advice.
