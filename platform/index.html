Add your own exercise description

Enter a description from a physiotherapy leaflet, a clinic letter, or in your own words. It will be separated into numbered steps, with any repetitions, hold times and safety notes identified. The original text is kept alongside so the two can be compared.

Full paragraphs are fine. Descriptions that are already numbered or bulleted will keep the structure you entered.
